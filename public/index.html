      Welcome · Feature Guide

      ✨ What's New in Household Finance?

      Here's what each page does and where to find it. All registered, verified Finance email accounts now use the same shared household records.

      🏠

      Household Dashboard

      See monthly income, expenses, and household totals for His, Hers, or Combined. Review and roll over your monthly budget.

      ✅

      My To-Do List · Menu → My To-Do List

      Each login has a private task list, separate from shared finances. New unfinished tasks prompt a Home reminder only until acknowledged.

      👤

      Finance Profiles · Menu → Profiles

      Create, rename, and switch profiles such as Personal or Business. Each profile has its own four trackers, and everyone in the household can use them.

      🧾

      Monthly Expense Tracker

      Record His, Hers and Together spending by month and category. Combined shows all purchases without double counting joint spending.

      💰

      Money Allocation

      Organize how money is assigned to goals and expenses. Choose a profile and switch between His and Hers allocations.

      💳

      Payment Plans

      Keep track of purchases paid over time, including the amount owed, installment schedule, and progress.

      🛍️

      Pay in 4 Tracker

      Record four-installment purchases, upcoming due dates, and what has already been paid.

      🛒

      Purchase Plan & Wishlist

      Plan purchases before spending, organize desired items, and track what you still want to buy.

      📅

      ESSO Due Dates

      Monitor ESSO payment deadlines and review the reminders available while the app is open.

      ☁️

      Shared Household Cloud

      All verified registered emails view and edit one household dataset. Use Refresh From Cloud and the backup tools in Settings to keep devices in sync safely.

      ⚙️

      Settings · Account & Security

      View your login profile, change your username or password, log out, configure reminders, and download or restore finance backups.

      📲

      Install & Notifications

      Install the dashboard as a web app and set up supported notifications. Alerts while the app is fully closed require a separate push sender.

      Household privacy: Any person who can register and confirm a Finance account can see and edit these shared records. Give account access only to people you trust.

      October 28, 2026 — Allocation by Percentage

      Create or edit an allocation as a percentage of the selected month’s average paycheck, with dollar amounts that update as pay changes. Totals also show the allocated and remaining percentages.

      October 28, 2026 — Liquid Glass Appearance

      A glass-inspired look adds translucent navigation and controls. Turn it on or off in Settings on each device; your financial data and cloud sync stay the same.

      October 28, 2026 — Expense Payment Methods

      You can now select how an expense was paid (Cash App, Chase Debit Card, Cash, etc.), create your own sources, rename/remove/restore them, filter and compare spending by method, and include methods in CSV exports. Older expenses retain their recorded source even after it is removed from new choices.

      October 27, 2026 — Latest Updates Popup

      The Home popup now shows only the most recent release changes instead of the full feature list. After pressing OK, Got It, that release stays acknowledged. All earlier changes remain in this complete Settings history, and the full Feature Guide is available separately on demand.

      October 27, 2026 — Expense Receipts & Payment Confirmation

      Attach PDF or image receipts to expenses (up to eight, 10 MB each). What I Owe now supports partial payments and paid-in-full confirmations, automatic remaining-balance updates and private payment history. Every payment has an OK acknowledgement popup.

      October 26, 2026 — Add-Entry Buttons and Update Popup

      To-Do and What I Owe information forms are now opened through Add buttons. The What's New popup announces this release once per signed-in account and browser, and older updates remain available here.

      October 26, 2026 — Private What I Owe Tracker

      Added a per-email What I Owe page for creditors, total owed, due dates, partial payments, remaining balances and paid-off tracking. Private across Finance logins, with optional one-time Home reminders when new unpaid entries are added.

      October 25, 2026 — Private Personal To-Do Lists

      Added My To-Do List in the Finance menu. Each registered email has its own private task list with due dates, priority, notes, completion and editing. Only new, unfinished tasks trigger the Home reminder; acknowledging them prevents repeat popups until more are added. Tasks sync across devices signed into that email, separately from shared household finances.

      October 23, 2026 — Username Sign-In Repair

      Updated the Supabase username-login Edge Function (including browser CORS) and added clearer sign-in error messages. Email and password sign-in continues to work as an alternative.

      October 22, 2026 — Smart Install Visibility

      When Household Finance runs as an installed PWA or Android APK, the in-page Install icon and installation cards no longer appear. The site also remembers successful PWA installation on that browser, and prompts return if the browser offers installation again after an uninstall.

      October 22, 2026 — Monthly Expense Tracker & Android

      Added His Spending, Hers Spending and Combined Spending, including Together expenses. Includes editable purchases, categories, monthly filtering, category totals and CSV download. Added one-time release announcements and Android APK build project.

      October 20, 2026 — Feature Guide

      Added the home-screen feature walkthrough with its Don't show again setting, plus reopening the guide from Settings.
